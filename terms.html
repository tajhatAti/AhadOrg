Terms of Service

Last updated · July 19, 2026  ·  applies to ahad-co.onrender.com

1The service

Ahad Co is a free code hosting platform. It gives you a browser code editor, published snippet pages, and RunSpace — a runner that keeps your apps, APIs and bots online with a public URL. It is built for developers, with Telegram bot developers as the primary audience.

The service is provided free of charge, on a best-effort basis, from Dhaka, Bangladesh.

2Free tier & fair use

  • 3 running jobs per account on the free tier, enforced server-side.
  • Every job runs under CPU, memory and runtime caps. Runaway processes are killed automatically.
  • Outbound traffic from jobs is rate-limited. High-volume or commercial workloads are not what the free tier is for.
  • We may reclaim capacity from jobs that idle, crash-loop, or misbehave.

3Acceptable use

Allowed: Telegram/Discord bots, small APIs, webhooks, learning projects, demos, scrapers that respect target sites' rules, and published code pages.

Not allowed — accounts are suspended for:

  • Spam, phishing, scams, malware, or credential/token theft of any kind
  • Denial-of-service activity, port scanning, or attacking other services
  • Crypto mining or any form of resource abuse
  • Hosting or proxying illegal content under Bangladeshi law or international law
  • Abusing platform resources, evading limits, or automating signups
  • Impersonating another person, product, or service

  • You are responsible for what your code does, including the third-party APIs and bot tokens it uses.

5Your account

  • One account per person. Do not create accounts in bulk.
  • Keep your password and recovery codes safe — we can reset access only through your verified email.

We review reports, and we may suspend accounts or remove content that breaks these terms, with or without notice when the abuse is severe. Suspension actions are recorded in an internal audit log.
